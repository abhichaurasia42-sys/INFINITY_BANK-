Customer Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last revised: October 2026 • Information Technology Act 2000 & RBI Cyber Security Framework Compliant

🔒

Your Privacy is Our Priority

At Infinity Bank, we are committed to upholding the highest standards of financial privacy, safeguarding your personal data, identity credentials, and financial history.

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-security digital banking and comply with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) KYC guidelines, we collect:

  • Personal Identity Information: Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, Aadhaar/PAN number, and demographic records.
  • Financial & Transaction Records: Account balances, transaction ledgers, beneficiary account details, bill payments, and recharge history.
  • Technical & Session Data: IP address, device fingerprints, browser security headers, and authentication timestamps.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

Your confidential data is utilized strictly for lawful banking purposes:

  • Execution and verification of domestic and international fund transfers (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI).
  • Real-time automated fraud detection and prevention algorithms.
  • Dispatching real-time transactional alerts and one-time passwords (OTP) via certified SMTP nodes.
  • Statutory reporting mandated under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

3. Data Protection & Cryptographic Standards

We deploy bank-grade cryptographic safeguards across our infrastructure:

  • End-to-end 256-Bit SSL/TLS data encryption in transit.
  • Salted cryptographic hashing of all passwords and security PINs.
  • Zero-knowledge access architecture ensuring employee access is governed by multi-tier authorization.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure Restrictions

Infinity Bank maintains an absolute policy against selling, renting, or trading customer data to commercial third parties. Information is disclosed solely to authorized regulatory clearing houses (NPCI, RBI, DICGC) or statutory enforcement authorities under valid legal warrants.

5. Data Retention & Grievance Redressal

Records are retained in accordance with RBI statutory retention regulations. If you have inquiries regarding your personal data or wish to file a grievance, please address our Data Protection Officer at infinitybank3@gmail.com.
